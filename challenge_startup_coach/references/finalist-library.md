# Finalist Library — 152 teams

Every finalist from the 2026 Diamond Challenge (83) and Conrad Challenge (33), all 22 Blue Ocean 2026 winners, and the 14 Conrad 2025 award winners. 62 won something, marked ★.

**What this is for.** When a student says their idea is too small, too obvious, or already taken, find the counterexample here. When they cannot picture what "good enough" looks like, show them the Proof lines. Sorted by theme so you can pull five adjacent precedents in seconds.

**Read the Proof lines as a group.** Almost every Conrad team has one. Most Diamond teams do not, because Diamond publishes no metrics. That contrast is itself the lesson about what each competition is asking for.
